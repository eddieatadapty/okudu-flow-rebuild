Day 1

Your name, checked

You hold 92% of US Apple Ads searches for your own name. Check who shows on the rest, and take that slot too.

Week 1

Two ad flows live

After a one-time SDK setup, each ad opens its own flow, set by where the install came from. No app release.

Week 2

A/B tests per ad

Each ad flow against one flow for everyone, measured on subscriptions, not installs. Then a look at broad keywords like “become”, “kind” and “fake”.

Both ads are live in public sources: the Meta one in the Meta Ad Library (page OKUDU, the App Store version running since 29 October 2026), the YouTube one on your “Okudu ai” channel. On Apple Ads, the US keywords we can see are account-level: you hold 92% of searches for your own name and 54% for “create stories”, while broad single words such as “become”, “kind” and “fake” bring searches with no clear wish to write a story. This layer carries no range: only a test per ad can give one.
